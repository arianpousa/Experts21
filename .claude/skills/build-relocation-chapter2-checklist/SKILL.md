# S42 · Build a relocation and Chapter 2 filing checklist

Used by: `act60-incentives-specialist`, `residency-relocation-advisor`

## Input

- Founder's situation (portfolio, current residence, family)
- Target move and filing dates

## Steps

1. Chapter 2 decision: complete application filed by 11:59 p.m. Dec 31, 2026 → 0% on dividends, interest and post-move gains to 2035; filed from Jan 1, 2027 → 4% to 2055. The DDEC portal fee-payment timestamp is the proof.
2. Eligibility: look-back (post-2026: no Puerto Rico residency in the prior 6 years).
3. Application: DDEC portal, $5,000 fee, required documents; counsel.
4. Residency steps: housing; home purchase within 2 years (registry recording for post-2026 applications); driver's license, voter registration, banks, doctors, schools, car; reduce mainland ties; day tracking.
5. Annual obligations: $10,000 donation (split as required), $5,000 annual report fee (filed with Hacienda from tax year 2026).
6. Federal points: pre-move gains (10-year rule), final state return, FBAR/FATCA.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Checks

- Not legal or tax advice: confirm with licensed counsel.
- The Dec 31, 2026 date is driven by filing, not moving.
