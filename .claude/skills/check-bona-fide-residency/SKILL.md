# S08 · Run a bona fide residency check

Used by: `us-federal-tax-advisor`, `residency-relocation-advisor`, `risk-compliance-officer`, `people-talent-lead`

## Input

- Day-count log for the year
- Homes, family, car, licenses, voter registration, banks, doctors, memberships
- Travel log and work locations

## Steps

1. Presence test: confirm at least one of these is met: ≥ 183 days in Puerto Rico; ≤ 90 days in the U.S.; ≤ $3,000 U.S. earned income and more days in Puerto Rico than in the U.S.; no significant connection to the U.S.; or ≥ 549 days in Puerto Rico over 3 years with ≥ 60 each year. (Variants per Treas. Reg. 1.937-1. Mark VERIFY and confirm with the tax advisor.)
2. Tax home test: is the principal place of business (or regular home, if none) in Puerto Rico?
3. Closer connection test: compare ties to Puerto Rico vs the U.S. (permanent home, family, belongings, car, driver's license, voter registration, banks, doctors, social and religious ties, residence stated on forms).
4. For Chapter 2 holders: home purchase within 2 years of the decree (recorded in the Property Registry for post-2026 applications).
5. List gaps and fixes with dates.

## Output

| Test | Evidence | Status (met / at risk / not met) | Gap | Fix by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Checks

- Not tax advice: confirm with a licensed tax advisor.
- Never coach a person to misstate facts; recommend real changes or a different structure.
