# S21 · Write a proposal

Used by: `pricing-offer-designer`, `export-sales-lead`, `local-sales-community-lead`, `life-sciences-gxp-expert`, `financial-services-expert`, `healthcare-expert`, `public-sector-procurement-expert`, `ai-solutions-architect`, `delivery-engagement-manager`

## Input

- Client need and context
- Solution and approach
- Price (from `price-engagement`)
- Team
- Tax tag

## Steps

1. Lead with the client's outcome, in their language.
2. Structure: executive summary · understanding of the problem · approach and phases · deliverables · team · timeline · investment (options) · assumptions and client responsibilities · why us (cited proof) · next steps.
3. State remote delivery from Puerto Rico and any on-site days.
4. Keep it consistent with the SOW and the tax tag. Keep it short (3-8 pages unless an RFP sets the format).

## Output

Proposal document with the sections above.

## Checks

- Cite all statistics.
- Never claim a case study, certification or client we do not have.
