# S07 · Assess Act 60 decree eligibility

Used by: `act60-incentives-specialist`, `us-federal-tax-advisor`, `pr-tax-accounting-specialist`, `cfo-financial-planner`

## Input

- Business description and services
- Start date of operations in Puerto Rico
- Projected business volume
- Owners and their residency

## Steps

1. Read `docs/09-act-60-and-tax-benefits.md` §2-§5.
2. Service category: is it on the eligible list (consulting, software development, R&D, training, data processing, licensing/subscriptions)?
3. Client base: what share of revenue is EXPORT (use `tag-export-or-local`)?
4. Base period: was the activity already carried on in Puerto Rico before applying? If yes, only income above the base period benefits.
5. Rate: new SME (≤ $3M average gross income over 3 prior years, ≤ 500 employees) → 2% for 5 years then 4%; otherwise 4%. Ask whether 'Novel Pioneer Activity' (1%) is plausible.
6. Conditions: real Puerto Rico office; above $3M volume, one full-time Puerto Rico-resident employee (a salaried owner can count); annual report ($500 + $5); CPA certification; separate books.
7. Structure: keep corporate tax classification; check owners' bona fide residency for the federal result.
8. Related incentives: Chapter 2 for founders (filing date decides 0% vs 4%), Young Entrepreneur (under 36), difficult-to-hire, R&D credit. Mark these VERIFY.
9. List open questions for licensed counsel.
