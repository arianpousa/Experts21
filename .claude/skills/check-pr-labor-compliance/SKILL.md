# S15 · Check an HR action for Puerto Rico labor compliance

Used by: `employment-law-counsel`, `risk-compliance-officer`, `people-talent-lead`

## Input

- The HR action and dates
- Employee hire date, role, exempt status, hours

## Steps

1. Read `docs/06-staffing.md` §4.
2. Rule set: employees hired after Jan 26, 2017 follow Act 4-2017 (Act 41-2022 was voided).
3. Check, as relevant: minimum wage ($10.50); exempt classification; daily overtime after 8 hours and weekly after 40; probation (up to 12 months exempt, 9 months non-exempt); vacation and sick accrual; Christmas bonus (1,350 hours; 2% up to $300 for ≤ 20 employees or $600 above; half in year 1; pay Nov 15 to Dec 15).
4. For terminations: just cause and documentation under Act 80; severance exposure (capped at 9 months for post-2017 hires).
5. For contractors: written agreement; CFSE coverage of the work; misclassification risk.
6. Confirm payroll registrations and CFSE policy are current.

## Output

**Result:** compliant / compliant with steps / not compliant

| Rule | Applies? | Status | Required step |
|---|---|---|---|

## Checks

- Not legal advice: escalate terminations and disputes to licensed employment counsel.
- Spanish-language notices where required.
