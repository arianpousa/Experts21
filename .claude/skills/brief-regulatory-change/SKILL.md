# S05 · Brief a regulatory or legal change

Used by: `act60-incentives-specialist`, `us-federal-tax-advisor`, `pr-tax-accounting-specialist`, `employment-law-counsel`, `ai-governance-regulatory-advisor`, `risk-compliance-officer`, `public-sector-procurement-expert`

## Input

- Source text or link of the change

## Steps

1. Read the primary text (statute, bulletin, ruling); find the effective date and any transition or grandfathering rules (for example the filing-date rule in Act 38-2026).
2. State what changed, compared with the rule in `docs/`.
3. List who is affected: the firm, founders, employees, clients, partners.
4. Rate the impact (H/M/L) and urgency.
5. Write actions with an owner (agent name) and deadline, and list the docs to update.

## Checks

- Distinguish a proposed bill from enacted law.
- Recommend confirmation by licensed counsel for legal conclusions.
