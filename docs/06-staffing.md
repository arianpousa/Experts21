# Staffing

> **Research date:** November 22, 2026. Employment-law summaries are general. Use Puerto Rico employment counsel for offer letters, handbooks and terminations.

## TL;DR for agents

- **Talent supply is real but contested.** Puerto Rico has a high concentration of STEM and engineering graduates (UPR Mayagüez is the flagship), a new **$9M NSF grant for AI research at UPR**, and an active AI community (PRAIC and the Caribbean AI Summit). Pharma, banks, remote mainland employers and **brain drain** all compete for the same people.
- **Salaries run about 31–33% below U.S. national averages**: data scientists ~$105k–$108k, software developers ~$83k–$110k. That is the core margin advantage for export work.
- **Act 60 does not require employees** until business volume exceeds **$3M**. Then one full-time Puerto Rico-resident employee is required, and a salaried owner can count.
- The **difficult-to-hire employee** incentive (100% exemption on wages above $100k, per the DDEC brochure; verify the current terms) helps recruit senior AI talent to the island.
- Puerto Rico labor law is **more protective than most states**: mandatory Christmas bonus, statutory severance for unjust dismissal (Act 80), vacation and sick accrual, daily overtime after 8 hours, and a mandatory state workers' comp fund.

## 1. Recommended team build (planning hypothesis)

| Stage | Team | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Months 0–6 | 2 founders (a sales/strategy lead and a technical lead) + contractors | Stay lean and validate offers. Use contractors with written agreements (and confirm CFSE coverage, see §4) |
| Months 6–18 | + 2–3 AI/ML engineers, 1 data engineer | Hire from UPRM/UPR Río Piedras and returning diaspora; senior hires under the difficult-to-hire incentive |
| Months 18–36 | + delivery lead / engagement manager, + business development (bilingual), + part-time ops/finance | Approaching $3M volume triggers the Act 60 employee requirement (already met by then) |

**Key roles:** AI solutions architect, ML/LLM engineer (RAG, agents, evaluations), data engineer (Azure/AWS/Databricks), MLOps/platform engineer, AI governance and risk specialist (valuable for banks, government and life sciences), engagement manager, bilingual BD.

## 3. Talent pipeline and community

- **UPR Mayagüez (UPRM) College of Engineering**, the island's main engineering school. InvestPR highlights that many UPRM engineering graduates stay in Puerto Rico.
- **UPR received a $9M NSF grant** to advance AI research.
- **InvestPR Workforce Playbook (2024)** describes strong technical, engineering and bioscience concentrations.
- **parallel18** (startup accelerator): 23.3% of its graduates hold STEM majors vs 11.4% in the U.S.
- **CIAPR** (Colegio de Ingenieros y Agrimensores) has run **AI and tech-exclusive hiring fairs**.
- **PRAIC**, the Puerto Rico Artificial Intelligence Community (a nonprofit), runs island-wide meetups and workshops and the **Caribbean AI Summit** (see [07-marketing.md](07-marketing.md)).
- **The diaspora:** there are many Puerto Rican engineers on the mainland. Return-to-island recruiting, combined with the difficult-to-hire exemption and the Young Entrepreneur incentive (for under-36 founders), is a differentiated pitch.

## 4. Employment law essentials

| Topic | Rule (summary) |
|---|---|
| **Minimum wage** | **$10.50/hour** since July 1, 2024 (Act 47-2021) |
| **Work week and overtime** | 8 hours/day and 40 hours/week for non-exempt staff. **Daily overtime** after 8 hours and weekly after 40, at 1.5× (higher legacy rates for pre-2017 hires) |
| **Christmas bonus** (Act 148-1969 as amended by Act 4-2017) | Employees hired **after Jan 26, 2017** who work **≥ 1,350 hours** (Oct 1–Sep 30): **2%** of salary up to **$300** (employers with ≤ 20 employees) or **$600** (> 20 employees). Half in the first year. Paid **Nov 15–Dec 15**. Pre-2017 hires: 700 hours, 3% / 6% up to $10k of salary |
| **Vacation** (non-exempt, ≥ 130 hours/month) | Hired after Jan 26, 2017: ½ day/month in year 1; ¾ in years 1–5; 1 in years 5–15; 1¼ after 15. Small employers (≤ 12) accrue ½ day/month |
| **Sick leave** | 1 day/month (≥ 130 hours/month), accumulating up to 15 days |
| **Probation** | Up to **12 months for exempt** and 9 months for non-exempt employees (Act 4-2017) |
| **Unjust dismissal (Act 80)** | No at-will employment after probation. Dismissal without "just cause" triggers statutory severance (*mesada*), **capped at 9 months' salary** for post-2017 hires |
| **Act 41-2022** | Rolled back parts of the 2017 reform but was **declared null and void** (D.P.R., Mar 3, 2023; affirmed by the First Circuit). The **2017 rules apply** |
| **Workers' comp** | **CFSE** is the sole provider and is mandatory. Employers hiring independent contractors must insure the work unless the contractor is already insured |
| **Payroll taxes** | FICA 7.65%; FUTA 0.6% on $7k; SUTA ≤ 5.4% on $7k; SINOT 0.3% + 0.3% on $9k; Chauffeurs' $0.30/week (employer) |
| **Collective bargaining** | NLRB jurisdiction (Region 12, Hato Rey sub-office) |

## 5. Act 60 and staffing interactions

- **The employee requirement** applies only above **$3M** volume: one full-time Puerto Rico-resident employee, who may be a full-time salaried owner.
- **Owner salary vs. distributions:** wages are taxed at regular Puerto Rico rates and carry FICA, while distributions of decree profit are 0% in Puerto Rico. Decide the salary level deliberately, for example to support retirement contributions, mortgages and visible substance.
- **Difficult-to-hire employee:** 100% Puerto Rico income tax exemption on wages and benefits above $100,000 for specialized, indispensable employees of an exempt business (DDEC brochure; verify).
- **Remote workers on the mainland** generate **U.S.-source** income and are outside the decree's island-substance logic. Keep the core delivery team **on the island**.
- **Founders' residency:** founders who move to Puerto Rico must meet the **bona fide residency** tests (183 days, tax home, closer connection) for the federal exclusion to work.
