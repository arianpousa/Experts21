# Startup and Operating Costs

> **Research date:** November 22, 2026. Government fees, tax rates, rents and salary benchmarks are sourced (see the bottom). Line items marked *(est.)* are planning estimates for the finance agent to refine with real quotes.

## TL;DR for agents

| Scenario | One-time startup | Annual operating (run-rate) | Suggested capital incl. runway |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Solo founder** (coworking, contractors as needed) | ~$15k–$40k | ~$60k–$120k (excl. founder pay) | ~$50k–$100k |
| **B. 5-person firm** (2 founders + 3 AI engineers) | ~$50k–$110k | ~$700k–$800k | ~$400k–$500k (6 months runway) |

The cost structure is driven by **people**. Puerto Rico tech salaries run about **31–33% below** U.S. national averages, while export work can be billed near mainland rates. **Taxes are low with a decree:** 2% on export profit for a new SME's first five years, plus property-tax and municipal-license exemptions.

**Employer payroll burden** (sourced rates; see [06-staffing.md](06-staffing.md)):

| Item | Rate / amount |
|---|---|
| FICA (Social Security + Medicare) | 7.65% (SS wage base $184,500 in 2026) |
| FUTA | 0.6% effective on the first $7,000 |
| Puerto Rico unemployment (SUTA) | Experience-rated plus a 1% special tax, combined ≤ 5.4%, on the first $7,000 |
| Disability (SINOT) | 0.3% employer + 0.3% employee on the first $9,000 |
| Chauffeurs' Social Security | $0.30 per employee per week (employer) |
| Workers' comp (State Insurance Fund, CFSE) | Mandatory, sole provider; premium by payroll class (low for office work) *(est. < 1%)* |
| Christmas bonus (employees hired after Jan 26, 2017) | 2% of salary, capped at **$300** (≤ 20 employees) or $600 (> 20); requires 1,350 hours |
| Health insurance | ~$400–$700 per employee per month *(est.)* |

A rule of thumb for total burden on top of salary is **~12–20%**, depending on health plan.

### 2.4 Taxes and government fees (sourced)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual LLC fee (Department of State) | **$150**, due by **April 15** (corporations file an annual report instead) |
| Act 60 Chapter 3 annual report | **$500 + $5** |
| Income tax on **export** profit (decree) | **4%**, or **2%** for 5 years if a new SME |
| Income tax on **local** profit | Regular corporate rates: 18.5% normal tax + surtax, **up to 37.5%**; AMT 18.5% (23% at ≥ $10M volume) |
| Distributions of decree profit | 0% Puerto Rico tax |
| Municipal license tax (*patente*) | **0.20%–0.50%** of gross volume, by municipality; **50% exempt** under a decree (**100% exempt for 5 years** as a new SME) |
| Personal property tax (CRIM) | ~5.80%–9.83% of assessed value, by municipality; **75% exempt** under a decree (100% for 5 years as a new SME) |
| IVU (sales and use tax) | 11.5% standard; **4%** on B2B and designated professional services; see [05-permits-and-licenses.md](05-permits-and-licenses.md) for exclusions |

## 3. Illustrative annual budget: Scenario B (5 people)

| Line | Annual |
|---|---|
| 3 AI engineers / data scientists ($110k + $100k + $85k) | $295,000 |
| 2 founders on salary ($75k each; optional above the $3M threshold, see Act 60) | $150,000 |
| Employer payroll taxes (~8.5%) | ~$38,000 |
| Health insurance (5 × ~$6k) | ~$30,000 |
| Christmas bonuses (5 × $300) | $1,500 |
| Office (coworking private office for 5) | ~$36,000 |
| Cloud, AI APIs, SaaS | ~$48,000 |
| Insurance (E&O, cyber, GL) | ~$15,000 |
| Accounting, legal, compliance | ~$36,000 |
| Marketing | ~$60,000 |
| Travel | ~$24,000 |
| Power and connectivity resilience | ~$6,000 |
| Miscellaneous | ~$15,000 |
| **Total operating** | **≈ $755,000** |

**Illustrative revenue** *(est.)*: 3 staff × 1,800 hrs × 65% utilization × $175/hr ≈ $614k, plus 2 founders × 1,800 hrs × 40% × $250/hr ≈ $360k, for about **$975k** and a **pre-tax profit of ~$220k**.

**Tax on that profit**, assuming a 70% export mix: export ~$154k × 2% ≈ **$3.1k**; local ~$66k at regular graduated rates ≈ $12k–$25k *(est.)*. Without a decree, the whole $220k could face rates of up to 37.5%.

Hourly rates are assumptions: mainland mid-market AI consulting at roughly **$175–$275/hr** for export work and **$100–$175/hr** for local work. The pricing agent should validate them.

## 4. Cost-control levers

- Stay below **$3M** business volume early to avoid the Act 60 employee requirement (or plan the salaried-owner route).
- Use **coworking** for the first 12–18 months.
- Bill **R&D and IP** work through the decree. Check eligibility for the **R&D credit** (up to 50%).
- Hire recent **UPR Mayagüez** graduates and train them. Use the **difficult-to-hire** exemption to make senior hires affordable.
- Pass-through LLM and cloud costs to clients with a margin.
