# Marketing and Go-to-Market

> **Research date:** November 22, 2026. Channels and events are sourced. Tactics and budgets are planning recommendations.

## TL;DR for agents

- Run **two go-to-market motions** that match the two tax tags (see [02-target-customers.md](02-target-customers.md)):
  1. **Local (Puerto Rico): relationship-led.** Business associations, events, the Microsoft partner ecosystem, bilingual thought leadership, and referrals. Puerto Rico business runs on relationships.
  2. **Export (mainland and LatAm): account-based outbound + content + partnerships.** The positioning is *"onshore U.S. AI team, bilingual, Eastern-time, nearshore pricing"*, plus life-sciences credibility.
- **Anchor events:** the **Caribbean AI Summit** (annual, October, Puerto Rico Convention Center, up to ~1,000 attendees), the **Cámara de Comercio's "Techno Tuesday"** AI sessions, and the **Colmena66** events calendar.
- **Brand promise:** *production AI with measurable ROI and governance built in.* It answers the main barrier IDC found in Puerto Rico, data readiness and security.

## 4. Messaging guardrails (tax and compliance)

- Contracts and SOWs for export clients should state the **client entity, where the operations benefited are located, and that work is performed from Puerto Rico**. This supports Act 60 eligibility and the sourcing of income.
- Do **not** promise on-site mainland presence as the default. Offer remote delivery with occasional visits, and track travel days (U.S.-source income and founder residency).
- Bilingual collateral should be **native-quality Spanish (Puerto Rico)**, not machine-translated.
