# Target Customers

> **Research date:** November 22, 2026. Segments are derived from the market data in [01-market.md](01-market.md) and the Act 60 rules in [09-act-60-and-tax-benefits.md](09-act-60-and-tax-benefits.md).

## TL;DR for agents

Every segment has a **tax tag**:
- **EXPORT:** a client outside Puerto Rico, for work with no Puerto Rico nexus. Taxed at the decree's **2–4%**.
- **LOCAL:** a Puerto Rico client, or work about Puerto Rico operations, law, government or property. Taxed at **regular rates** of up to 37.5%.

The recommended mix is an **export-led core** (mainland mid-market and life sciences) for margin and tax efficiency, plus a **local beachhead** (Puerto Rico enterprises, SMEs, government) for reputation, case studies and bilingual credibility.

## Qualification and tax-tagging rules (for sales and finance agents)

1. **Where is the client legal entity located?** If in Puerto Rico, tag LOCAL.
2. **Which operations benefit from the work?** If they are in Puerto Rico (for example a mainland parent's Puerto Rico plant), tag LOCAL, or split the SOW.
3. **Does the work involve Puerto Rico law or regulation, Puerto Rico government lobbying, or property used in Puerto Rico?** If so, tag LOCAL.
4. Otherwise tag **EXPORT** and document the rationale in the client nexus register.
5. **Where will the work be delivered?** On-site days on the mainland create **U.S.-source** income. Prefer remote delivery from Puerto Rico and record travel.
