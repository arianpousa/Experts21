# Biggest Risks

> **Research date:** November 22, 2026. Ratings are qualitative judgments for planning (L/M/H).

## TL;DR for agents

The five biggest risks, in order:
1. **Act 60 eligibility and income-sourcing errors.** Treating local work as export, doing work off the island, or electing pass-through tax status can wipe out the tax advantage or trigger penalties.
2. **IRS and congressional scrutiny of Puerto Rico incentives.** The GAO (Dec 2025) told the IRS to tighten oversight, and Senator Wyden (Apr 2026) called for a full investigation. Founders' **bona fide residency** must be airtight.
3. **Demand mismatch.** The tax-advantaged market (off-island clients) has to be won from Puerto Rico against mainland and LatAm competitors. The local market is easier to enter but taxed at regular rates, and many small businesses there have low readiness.
4. **Talent competition and brain drain** for senior AI engineers.
5. **Infrastructure and climate:** grid outages, high electricity cost and hurricanes.

## Federal scrutiny: what the record shows

- **GAO-26-107225** (Dec 2025):
  - About **2,200** resident-investor incentive recipients in 2021.
  - Almost **4,000** export-services incentive recipients from 2012–2024.
  - Recipients were on average high-income and **paid significantly less federal tax** after moving.
  - The IRS campaign started in 2021 and only recently produced results.
  - GAO made **three recommendations**: regular data from Hacienda, written procedures for Puerto Rico referrals, and educational letters to recipients.
- Analysis of the GAO data: **5,852** resident-investor decrees were granted from 2012–2024, but only **2,236** holders filed Puerto Rico returns in 2021. Average federal taxable income fell 39% after relocating.
- **Senate Finance (Wyden), Apr 30, 2026:** called for a full IRS investigation of a tax shelter involving improper sourcing of income to Puerto Rico.
- **Implication:** an AI consulting firm with real island operations, local employees and clean sourcing is the *intended* beneficiary of Chapter 3. Documentation is what makes the difference in an audit.
