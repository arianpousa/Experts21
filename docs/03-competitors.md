# Competitors

> **Research date:** November 22, 2026. The landscape comes from public listings and directories (MolecularCloud "Top 10 AI Development Companies in Puerto Rico 2026", Clutch, The Manifest, LinkedIn, company sites). These directories are partly pay-to-play, so verify each firm before relying on details.

## TL;DR for agents

- The **local** Puerto Rico AI market is **fragmented**. It consists mostly of small boutiques and software shops, many focused on chatbots, WhatsApp and voice agents for SMEs, plus a few enterprise-grade engineering firms (Xtillion, Enalca, Involve, Intelligent Economics).
- At the top end the competitors are the **Big 4** (Deloitte, PwC, EY and KPMG all have San Juan offices) and **Microsoft partners**. On the export side the firm competes with **every mainland boutique and nearshore LatAm firm**.
- **White space:** (1) GxP-aware AI for life sciences with Puerto Rico plant experience, (2) a **bilingual, U.S.-jurisdiction, Act 60-advantaged** export consultancy that sells to the mainland mid-market, and (3) AI **governance and data-readiness** work for Puerto Rico enterprises and government ahead of the proposed AI rules.

## Positioning recommendations

1. **Lead with outcomes and production, not demos.** Many local firms sell chatbots. Differentiate on data foundations, evaluation, security and measurable ROI, which answers the "data structure" barrier IDC found.
2. **Vertical depth:** life sciences (GxP), banking and insurance (model risk, compliance), healthcare (HIPAA).
3. **Dual brand narrative.** *Locally:* "Puerto Rican AI talent building for Puerto Rico." *Export:* "Onshore U.S. AI team, bilingual, Eastern time, nearshore pricing."
4. **Partner rather than fight:** subcontract to the Big 4 and IT incumbents on Puerto Rico RFPs, and co-sell with Microsoft.
